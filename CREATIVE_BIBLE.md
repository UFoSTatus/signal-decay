## CONCEPT

A melodic metalcore band where each member possesses **dormant supernatural abilities** tied to their instrument. They live normal lives — dead-end jobs, broken relationships, quiet desperation — until **one day** something triggers an awakening. When they play, reality bends. People can't explain why the music hits different. It's not just talent. It's something *else*.

The world doesn't know yet. But it will.

---

## TONE & THEMES

- **Genre:** Melodic metalcore / post-hardcore (Think: Killswitch Engage meets Spiritbox meets Underoath)
- **Lyrical themes:** Awakening, isolation, power you can't control, finding your tribe, the weight of being "chosen"
- **Visual aesthetic:** Dark, ethereal, glowing elements (like bioluminescence), urban decay mixed with otherworldly light
- **Narrative arc:** Origin → Discovery → Struggle → Unity → Unleashed
