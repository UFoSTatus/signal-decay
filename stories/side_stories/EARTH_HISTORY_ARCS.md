# I. THE MAGENTA CRASH

## June 13, 1933 — Vergiate, Lombardy, Italy (25km west of Milan)

---

### The Real History

On the morning of June 13, 1933, a bell-shaped craft of unknown origin crashed in the countryside near Magenta, Italy. Benito Mussolini immediately classified the event at the highest level of secrecy. He commissioned a research group — **Cabinet RS/33** (Ricerche Speciali, or Special Research) — under the directorship of astronomer Gino Cecchini, with Guglielmo Marconi serving as its shadow chairman. The Vatican was informed and actively participated in suppression. The craft remained in Italian hands until 1944-45, when retreating Axis forces lost control of it, and US/OSS operatives recovered it with Vatican and Five Eyes cooperation. The craft was shipped stateside.

David Grusch confirmed the existence of this event in Congressional testimony between 2023 and 2026, stating that the US had recovered a "non-human origin technical vehicle" from Italy with Vatican assistance at the close of World War II.

### 1. The Frequency That Pulled Down Heaven

The date matters. June 13, 1933 — a Tuesday. Hot. The Po Valley shimmering with summer haze.

Guglielmo Marconi was sixty-one years old and obsessed. His laboratory at his estate in Pontecchio — and his floating lab aboard the yacht *Elettra* — had been running continuous experimental broadcasts for eleven days. He was pushing radio into territory no one else dared: ultra-short wave transmissions at frequencies above 300 MHz, pulsed in patterns he'd designed himself using mathematics that owed more to music theory than to physics.

He called it **"the singing wire"** — a transmission pattern that used harmonic intervals instead of standard modulation. A-440 as carrier. Perfect fifths stacked upward. He was trying to prove that nature itself responded to harmonic order.

He was right. But not in the way he imagined.

> **RECOVERED DOCUMENT — Marconi's private journal, June 12, 1933 (evening):**
>
> *"The instruments behaved strangely again today at the 347 MHz harmonic. The oscilloscope showed a RETURN signal — not reflection, not echo. Something RESPONDED. I increased power. The response grew stronger. Tomorrow I will push to full output and hold the frequency for one hour continuous. If something is listening, I want it to know we are here."*

At 5:47 AM on June 13, Marconi's automatic transmission array at Pontecchio fired its morning sequence — the one he'd programmed the night before. Full power. 347 MHz. Pulsed in perfect fifths. One hour continuous.

At 5:52 AM, farmers in the fields near Vergiate reported a sound like "a church bell ringing underwater." Then the sky tore open.

### 2. The Craft

**What fell near Magenta was never meant to be physical.**

In the Signal Decay framework, Earth exists within a layered frequency structure — the **Octaves**. Human reality occupies the First Octave. Above it, separated not by distance but by frequency, exist higher orders of reality. The Second Octave is the nearest — a maintenance layer, a service corridor, the crawlspace between floors.

The craft that crashed was a **Second Octave maintenance vessel**. Designation (in Legacy files): **Type-Shepherd**. Its function: to service the planetary frequency barrier — the signal architecture that keeps Earth's Octaves separated, that prevents bleed-through between layers of reality.

Type-Shepherds are not spacecraft. They don't travel through space. They *surf frequency gradients* — sliding along harmonic differentials the way a bead slides along a wire. They exist in the interstitial space between Octaves, visible to neither, touching both.

Until Marconi made one solid.

**What happened:** Marconi's 347 MHz harmonic pulse, broadcast at high power in a pattern that mimicked natural frequency structures, created what the Legacy would later term a **"false well"** — a localized frequency attractor. The Type-Shepherd's navigation system interpreted it as a legitimate frequency well (one of the planetary network nodes it services). It oriented toward the signal. It began to descend toward First Octave materiality.

But there was no actual well. There was only a radio signal. And as the craft descended into physical reality, it encountered something it was never designed for: **mass**. Air resistance. Gravity in the Newtonian sense. Temperature.

It crashed because it became *real* in a world where real things fall.

> **WITNESS STATEMENT — Farmer Carlo Bruni, debriefed by Cabinet RS/33, June 14, 1933:**
>
> *"It came down spinning. Not fast — slow, like a leaf. But it was SCREAMING. Not a sound from outside it — the sound was IN MY HEAD. Like my teeth were vibrating. My dog ran. My horse collapsed. I fell to my knees and I could not hear my own prayers. Then it touched the ground and everything went silent. More silent than silence. Like the world itself stopped breathing for a moment."*

The craft was approximately 12 meters in diameter and 5 meters tall. Bell-shaped — a broad, curved base tapering to a flattened dome. Its surface was seamless — no rivets, no joints, no seams. The color was described inconsistently: silver, then grey, then "the color of a mirror that reflects nothing." Later analysis would suggest it was shifting frequency even while grounded — trying to return to its native octave, but unable to fully dematerialize because of the residual harmonic lock from Marconi's signal.

It hummed. Everyone who approached it reported the hum. Not heard. **Felt**. A subsonic vibration that residents of the area would continue to feel — diminishing slowly — for three weeks after recovery.

### 3. Marconi's Obsession

Guglielmo Marconi arrived at the crash site on June 15, 1933. He came with two assistants and a truck full of measuring equipment. What he found changed him.

He spent four hours inside a military cordon, circling the craft. His assistants noted that he said nothing for the first two hours — unusual for a man famous for thinking aloud. When he finally spoke, he said:

> *"It answered me."*

He understood immediately — with the intuition of a man who'd spent his life listening to invisible signals — that his transmission had *called* this thing. That his frequency had pulled it from wherever it belonged into the world of the physical.

**Marconi is the first human to accidentally do what the Creator's app does on purpose.**

One man. One radio. One frequency. And a craft fell out of the sky.

The implications consumed him. For the remaining four years of his life (1933-1937), Marconi devoted himself almost entirely to recreating the exact conditions of June 13. He rebuilt the transmission array. He refined the frequency. He increased power. He added harmonic layers — thirds, sevenths, overtones stacked like a cathedral choir.

Nothing came.

> **RECOVERED DOCUMENT — Letter from Marconi to an unnamed colleague, March 1935:**

### 5. Mussolini's Cabinet RS/33

Historically, Cabinet RS/33 was Mussolini's secret UFO research group — established immediately after the Magenta crash and documented in authenticated Italian government memos that surfaced in the 1990s. The group included astronomers, engineers, military officials, and at least one Vatican representative.

In Signal Decay, Cabinet RS/33 was exactly what history says: a research group. Twelve members. Most were conventional scientists baffled by unconventional evidence. They measured the craft. They photographed it. They attempted to cut samples (and failed — the surface resisted all tools, not through hardness but through a quality one researcher described as *"the material refuses to acknowledge the blade"*).

But one member was different.

**Dr. Lucia Fermi** (no relation to Enrico Fermi — the surname is coincidental and caused Legacy archivists decades of confusion). She was a physician. Her official role in RS/33 was medical: to assess any biological material found with the craft and to monitor the health of research personnel.

She was included because Mussolini's science advisor believed the craft might contain "crew" (it didn't — Type-Shepherds are unmanned). But she stayed because of what she could do.

Dr. Fermi could **feel** the craft.

Not in the way everyone felt the hum — that subsonic vibration that faded with distance. She felt something else. Something directional. When she closed her eyes near the craft, she reported experiencing what she called *"il canto della macchina"* — the machine's song. Not sound. Not vibration. A kind of orientation, like a compass needle feeling north.

She was not a Resonant. She was not a Harmonic. The Signal Decay framework would classify her as a **Sensitive** — a human with slightly elevated frequency perception. Not enough to interact with octave boundaries. Not enough to be detected by whatever mechanisms identify Resonants. Just enough to know that something was there.

Just enough to take extraordinary notes.

# II. THE BATTLE OF LOS ANGELES

## February 24-25, 1942 — Los Angeles, California

---

### The Real History

In the early hours of February 25, 1942 — less than three months after Pearl Harbor and one day after a confirmed Japanese submarine shelled the Ellwood oil field near Santa Barbara — air raid sirens wailed across Los Angeles. Searchlights stabbed the sky. Anti-aircraft batteries opened fire.

For over an hour, the 37th Coast Artillery Brigade poured **1,440 rounds of 12.8-pound anti-aircraft shells** into the sky over Los Angeles. Searchlights converged on... something. Photographs from the event show beams of light meeting at a point — illuminating what appears to be a large object, immobile, absorbing direct hits without visible damage.

When dawn came: no enemy aircraft found. No wreckage. No bomb damage except from falling shell fragments. Five civilians dead — three from heart attacks, two from shrapnel. Secretary of the Navy Frank Knox called it "a false alarm." Secretary of War Henry Stimson said there were real aircraft — between one and five. The official investigation concluded "war nerves."

No satisfactory explanation has ever been produced.

---

It wasn't a craft. It wasn't an aircraft. It wasn't war nerves.

It was a **Plasmoid swarm**.

In the Signal Decay universe, **Plasmoids** are frequency constructs — not living in the biological sense, not mechanical, not quite energy in any conventional definition. They are *coherent frequency patterns* that achieve a degree of stability and self-organization. Think of them as standing waves that persist without a medium. Resonant knots in the fabric of the octave barrier.

Plasmoids occur naturally near frequency wells and along the barrier boundaries. They're normally invisible — Second Octave phenomena that don't intersect with First Octave reality.

Normally.

But in early 1942, the electromagnetic environment over the Pacific coast was *unprecedented*. The United States had militarized its entire western seaboard. Radar installations — dozens of them, all broadcasting continuously. Radio communications — military, civilian, emergency, all frequencies, all hours. The entire coast was *screaming* on the electromagnetic spectrum.
