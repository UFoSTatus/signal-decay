# WHAT SHE SEES — Spirit Classification Guide
## How She Learned to Tell Them Apart (No One Taught Her)

*A reference document for "The Girl Who Sees" — the categories she figured out on her own, through years of watching, listening, and sometimes being terrified.*

---

> *"Nobody gave me a book. Nobody sat me down and explained it. I just... watched. For years. And after a while, you start to notice patterns. Like — oh, THIS kind doesn't see me. And THAT kind does. ## THE FIVE TYPES (As She Understands Them)

She doesn't use the fancy paranormal investigator terms. She made up her own names as a kid. But they map perfectly to the established classifications.

---

## 1. "THE LOOPS" (Residual Hauntings)

**What they are:** Not actually spirits. More like... recordings. An emotional moment so intense it got stamped into the place itself — the walls, the floor, the ground. It plays back on repeat like a video stuck on loop.

**How she figured it out:** At age 8, she saw a woman walk down the hallway of her school every single day at 2:15 PM. Same path. Same pace. Same sad expression. She tried to talk to her for WEEKS. The woman never looked at her. Never reacted. Never changed. Finally she realized: this isn't a person. This is a replay. Like watching a movie that plays itself.

**What they look like:**
- Do the same thing every time, never vary
- Don't react to anything — you can wave, yell, stand in their path
- Often slightly transparent or faded, like an old photograph
- Tied to ONE specific location — never follow you
- Usually connected to something emotional: a death, extreme grief, a traumatic moment
- Play on a schedule (same time of day, same weather conditions, anniversaries)

**How she feels about them:** They used to scare her (age 8-9). Now they make her sad. They're like echoes of pain that got stuck. She can't help them — there's nobody TO help. They're just... residue.

**What she does:** Nothing. You can't talk to a recording. She just acknowledges them quietly and moves on. Sometimes she whispers "I see you" even though they can't hear. It feels right.

**Signal Decay connection:** These are frequency imprints — emotional energy intense enough to embed in physical matter at a specific resonant frequency. The same principle as soul-imbued instruments, but for locations instead of objects.

---

## 2. "THE STUCK ONES" (Intelligent Hauntings — Confused/Lost)

**What they are:** Actual spirits. Real people who died and... didn't move on. Some don't know they're dead. Some know but are confused about what to do. Some are just... lingering. Attached to a place or a person or an unfinished thing.

**How she figured it out:** The first one who LOOKED BACK at her. She was 8. A man sitting on her porch. She said "hi" and he turned and said "Can you see me?" His face — the shock, the relief, the desperation. That's when she understood: these ones are AWARE. They know she can see them. And they're so lonely.

**What they look like:**
- They RESPOND. They look at you. React to what you say. Answer questions.
- Appear more solid than Loops — closer to how they looked alive
- Have PERSONALITY. Moods. Good days and bad days.
- Often confused about time ("What year is it?" is a question she's been asked dozens of times)
- Tied to places OR people, but can sometimes move around
- Some don't know they're dead. These are the hardest.

**What they want:**
- To be SEEN. Most of all. Just acknowledged.
- To deliver a message (tell my daughter, tell my wife, tell them I'm sorry)
- To understand what happened to them
- To be told it's okay to go
- Sometimes just... company. They're lonely.

**How she handles them:** 
- She talks to them. Normally. Like they're people. Because they ARE.
- She learned (age 10) that she can tell them to leave if they're being too much. Firmly. Like setting a boundary. "You need to go now. I'll talk to you tomorrow."
- She learned (age 11) that some need PERMISSION to leave. They're waiting for someone to say "it's okay. You can go." When she says it — some of them light up and just... dissolve. Peacefully. Those moments are beautiful.
- The ones who don't know they're dead — she has to be careful. Telling someone they're dead is... heavy. She's learned to be gentle. She was NOT gentle the first time (age 9, blurted it out, the spirit SCREAMED).

**The hard part:** They come to her. She didn't ask for them. They find out she can see them and they come. At school. At the store. In her bedroom at 3 AM. She had to learn to set HOURS. Like office hours. "I can talk between 4 and 6 PM. Not at night. Not at school." Some respect it. Some don't.

---

## 3. "THE ANGRY ONES" (Intelligent Hauntings — Hostile/Malevolent)

**What they are:** Spirits who are aware, intelligent, and MAD. Not confused — angry. Some were bad people in life. Some became bad through decades of being stuck and furious. Some are angry at specific people. Some are angry at everyone.

**How she figured it out:** Age 11. The basement of the public library. Something down there that didn't want to be seen and didn't want her there. It wasn't sad or lost — it was HOSTILE. The temperature dropped. The lights flickered. And she felt something she'd never felt before: hatred. Directed AT her. For the first time, a spirit didn't want help. It wanted her to LEAVE. Or worse.

**What they look like:**
- Darker. Literally — less light around them. Shadows seem to lean toward them.
- Features are harder to make out (like they're refusing to be seen clearly)
- The air around them feels WRONG — cold, heavy, electric
- They can affect the physical world more than the others — doors, lights, objects
- They manifest in places of violence, injustice, rage

**Warning signs she learned:**
- Temperature drop that feels intentional (not just cold — HOSTILE cold)
- A feeling of being watched with malice (different from a lonely spirit wanting attention)
- Physical reactions: nausea, headache, sudden exhaustion, chest pressure
- Animals react (dogs growl, cats hiss, birds go silent)
- Electronic interference (phone dies, lights flicker, speakers crackle)

**What she does:**
- She LEAVES. First and most important rule she learned: you don't have to stay.
- She learned (age 12) that she can push back. She can project her own energy — a "get BACK" that most angry ones respect. Not all. But most.
- She does NOT engage. Doesn't talk to them. Doesn't try to help them. Doesn't acknowledge them. Attention is what they want. Denying it is her best defense.
- The really bad ones: she avoids the places they inhabit. There's a house three blocks from her school she will NEVER walk past. She crosses the street every single time.

**The lesson:** Not every spirit deserves her help. Not every dead person was a good person. Death doesn't make you kind. Some people were cruel in life and they're cruel in death. She doesn't owe them anything.

## 4. "THE LOUD ONES" (Poltergeists)

**What they are:** The most PHYSICAL type. They move things. Break things. Throw things. Make noise. They're not tied to a place — they're tied to a PERSON. Usually someone young, emotional, going through something intense. The energy comes FROM the living person, even though it manifests as something that seems like a ghost.

**How she figured it out:** Age 12. Her classmate Maya started having "incidents." Her locker would slam open. Papers flew off her desk. A cup LAUNCHED across the cafeteria when Maya was upset about something. Everyone thought it was a ghost in the school. SHE knew it wasn't. Because she could SEE — there was no spirit. The energy was coming from MAYA. Pouring off her like heat off asphalt. Maya was going through her parents' divorce and her body was... leaking. Frequency energy with nowhere to go.

**What they look like:**
- There's often NO visible spirit at all — just effects. Things moving. Noise.
- If she focuses, she can sometimes see the energy — like heat shimmer, but more structured
- Centers on a specific person (usually a teenager, usually in emotional turmoil)
- Activity follows that person, not the location
- Starts small (things falling, electronics glitching) and escalates if the emotional source isn't addressed
- Can stop suddenly when the person processes whatever they're going through

**Key difference from ghosts:** Nobody's dead here. This is a living person's energy manifesting externally because they can't process it internally. It's not a haunting — it's an OVERFLOW.

**What she does:**
- She can't "fix" a poltergeist by talking to a spirit — there IS no spirit.
- What she CAN do: she can see the energy source. She knows WHO is generating it.
- Age 12-13, she started quietly helping. Not saying "you're doing poltergeist stuff" (that would go great in middle school). Instead: being kind to that person. Being a friend. Helping them process what they're going through. The activity calms as the person calms.
- She realized: SHE might have been a poltergeist source at some point too. When she was 9 and overwhelmed — things fell off shelves around her. Her mom's phone cracked. She didn't know it was her. Now she manages her own emotional energy more carefully.

**Signal Decay connection:** Poltergeist activity is essentially uncontrolled frequency emission — a Sensitive (or potential Sensitive) whose emotional state generates physical effects. It's the same mechanism as the Five's abilities, just chaotic and unconscious rather than directed.

## 5. "THE DARK ONES" (Inhuman / Demonic / Non-Human Entities)

**What they are:** Things that were NEVER human. Never lived. Never died. They just... are. They exist in the frequency spectrum but they don't come from a person's death or a person's emotions. They're something else entirely. She doesn't fully understand them yet. She's 13 and she knows enough to know she doesn't know enough.

**How she figured it out:** She's only encountered one. Age 12. In the old house on Meridian Street that nobody lives in. She went in on a dare (stupid, she knows). What she felt wasn't a dead person's anger or sadness or confusion. It was something that had no human emotion AT ALL. Cold. Vast. Ancient. Not angry — just... aware of her in a way that made her feel like an insect being examined.

**What they look/feel like:**
- No human form. Ever. If something inhuman LOOKS human, that's a choice it's making. That's worse.
- A presence that feels OLDER than any ghost she's encountered (ghosts feel decades old at most — this felt like centuries)
- No emotional residue she can read (with human spirits, she feels their grief, anger, love — with this: NOTHING. Just cold awareness)
- The air doesn't just get cold — it gets EMPTY. Like sound itself is being removed from the room.
- Her body reacted before her mind did: every hair standing up, nausea, a primal "RUN" signal she couldn't ignore

**What she does:**
- She ran. Immediately. No hesitation.
- She has NEVER gone back to that house.
- Her rule: if it doesn't feel human, she does not engage. Period. End of story. She's 13 and she knows she is not equipped for that.
- She suspects these things are connected to something bigger — something she'll understand when she's older. (Signal Decay: this was a Silence fragment. A tiny piece of the non-sound that predates everything. She is correct to be terrified.)

## WHAT SHE LEARNED ON HER OWN (No Mentor, No Guide)

She figured all of this out through trial and error. Some lessons were gentle. Some were terrifying. Here's her timeline of understanding:

**Age 8:** "Everyone can see these people, right?" (Wrong.)
**Age 8.5:** "Oh. They're dead. Only I can see them." (Terror.)
**Age 9:** "Some of them can talk to me. Some can't. What's the difference?" (Loops vs. Intelligent.)
**Age 9:** "If I tell people about this, they think I'm crazy." (Learns to hide it.)
**Age 10:** "I can tell them to leave. They listen. Most of the time." (Boundary setting.)
**Age 10:** "They come to ME. I didn't choose this." (Accepts the weight.)
**Age 11:** "Not all of them are nice." (First angry encounter.)
**Age 11:** "I can push back. I have some kind of... force." (Discovers she can project.)
**Age 11:** "Some need permission to go. When I give it — they're free." (Learns to release.)
**Age 12:** "Some aren't ghosts at all — they're energy from living people." (Poltergeists.)
**Age 12:** "Something exists that was never human. I'm not ready for that." (The Dark Ones.)
**Age 12:** "There are others like me online. I'm not the only one." (Community.)
**Age 13:** "This isn't a curse. But it IS a responsibility. And I'm getting stronger." (Acceptance.)
**Age 13:** "Something changed. All of them felt it. Something is SHIFTING." (The Signal.)

## HER RULES (Written in a notebook, purple pen, stickers on the cover)

1. I don't have to help every single one. I'm allowed to say no.
2. Not at night. NEVER at night anymore. Daytime only.
3. If it feels wrong — LEAVE. Don't wait. Don't investigate. Leave.
4. The angry ones feed on attention. Ignore them.
5. The sad ones just want to be seen. That's usually enough.
6. Never tell someone they're dead unless you're ready for their reaction.
7. Poltergeists aren't ghosts. Help the person, not the "spirit."
8. If it was never human — RUN.
9. Eating helps. Something about being full makes them quieter. (She doesn't know why. It's grounding.)
10. I am not broken. I am not crazy. I just see more than other people.
