## PART 8: THE SOUL — ORI-TAHN (Who He Was)

### The Timekeeper

In the pre-physical realm, Ori-Tahn was a TIMEKEEPER. Not a ruler. Not a warrior. MAINTENANCE. The mechanic who kept reality's clock running.

His function: maintaining temporal coherence across a cluster of 400 worlds. His rhythm was their rhythm. When he vibrated in his natural state, 400 planets orbited. Seasons changed. Tides moved. Cells divided at the rate he set.

He was ESSENTIAL. Without a Timekeeper, worlds drift. Seasons fail. Biology loses coherence. Things die at wrong times. Cause-and-effect frays.

### Why He Left

A sound reached him through dimensional layers. From a planet in his cluster called Earth. A rhythm produced by PHYSICAL BEINGS using PHYSICAL INSTRUMENTS. Imprecise. Sloppy. Emotionally chaotic.

And it was the most beautiful thing he'd encountered in 10 million cycles.

He wanted to know what it FELT like. To produce rhythm INSIDE a body. To feel a stick hit a drum THROUGH hands. To experience imperfection.

So he left. His 400 worlds were reassigned (poorly — three had ecological failures. One species went extinct from a breeding-season temporal drift). Ori-Tahn incarnated on Earth.

Timekeepers generate temporal coherence — and temporal DISRUPTION is attracted to temporal coherence. Like lightning to a rod. The bigger the drummer, the bigger the storm that finds them.

### His Base Rhythm: 7/8
