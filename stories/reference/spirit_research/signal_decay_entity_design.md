Multiple filament-like tendrils trail from its lower body, each one tipped with a crystalline node that pulses indigo when feeding.

**Behavior:** The Keening Latch is drawn to sustained grief—funerals, hospitals, disaster sites. **Frequency Range:** 27.5–33 Hz (sub-bass mourning spectrum)

**How Humans Perceive Them:** A sudden heaviness in the chest. The feeling that grief is "pressing down." Cold spots near the bereaved. Some sensitives report seeing a dark shimmer in peripheral vision. **Signal Decay Plot Connection:** Keening Latches congregate around Sunny after significant losses. Her Thanatological sight allows her to see them clearly, but their presence near her makes her grief over past events functionally permanent—she cannot process loss normally because they won't let her. The Dampener's suppression of grief-processing frequencies has made these creatures epidemic in hospitals and nursing homes.

*Not ghosts—consciousness patterns that replay. The scientific basis for residual hauntings. When a consciousness experiences extreme frequency output (trauma, ecstasy, terror), it can "burn" a pattern into the local frequency substrate, which then replays indefinitely like a groove in a record.*

### 4a. Trauma Impressions

**Name:** Trauma Impressions  
**Folklore Inspiration:** The residual haunting concept in parapsychology—the "stone tape theory" that suggests emotional events can be recorded in the fabric of a location.

**Physical Description:** Not visible under normal conditions. When perceived (by sensitives or frequency-detection equipment), they appear as semi-transparent replays of a past event—a figure walking the same path, performing the same actions, experiencing the same moment of extreme emotion. They have no depth or substance; they are two-dimensional projections encoded in the local frequency substrate (typically stone, iron-rich materials, or water).

**Behavior:** Purely repetitive. They have no consciousness, no awareness, no ability to interact. They are recordings—nothing more. The "person" they depict may or may not still be alive; it's irrelevant to the Echo's function. They replay when local frequency conditions match the original recording conditions (time of day, electromagnetic weather, lunar tidal effects on local frequency).

**Danger Level:** None physically. Psychologically disturbing to sensitives who perceive them as real people. Occasionally mistaken for intelligent hauntings, leading to misguided attempts at communication.

**Frequency Range:** Variable—matches the emotional frequency of the original event (typically 19–200 Hz for traumatic impressions)

**How Humans Perceive Them:** The classic "ghost sighting" that repeats the same action, doesn't respond to interaction, and is seen at the same time/location repeatedly. [Infrasound and Haunting](https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/04/that-spooky-sensation-likely-due-to-rumbling-pipes-not-spirits/){evidence: "The next time you walk into a purportedly 'haunted' house and sense a ghostly presence, consider that those feelings might be due to vibrating pipes, mechanical or climate control systems, rumbling from traffic, or wind turbines, rather than anything paranormal."}

**Signal Decay Plot Connection:** The Dampener's frequency suppression is causing Trauma Impressions to "bleed" into perceptibility for normal humans—previously only sensitives could detect them. This is causing a global increase in ghost sightings, which the conspiracy must manage. The Five can intentionally "read" Trauma Impressions for information about past events at locations.

## Summary

### Key Takeaways

1. **Every folklore tradition encodes frequency reality.** The Signal Decay universe doesn't debunk mythology—it reveals that myths were early humanity's attempts to describe frequency phenomena they could perceive but not measure. Parasitic spirits, ocean gods, angels, demons, ghost lights—all are frequency entities observed through different cultural lenses.

2. **The entity taxonomy maps to narrative function.** Frequency Parasites create personal stakes (the Five must manage their emotional output). Silence Fragments create existential stakes (reality itself is threatened). Well Guardians and Octave Travelers create scale (forces beyond human comprehension are in play). Dampener Aberrations create urgency (the problem is getting worse by generating new threats).

3. **The Dampener is an ecological disaster.** Like pollution creating cancer in biological ecosystems, the Dampener has corrupted the frequency ecosystem—spawning unnatural entities (Static Crawlers, Frequency Cysts), overpopulating parasites (by suppressing humans' natural defenses), and weakening ancient protective systems (the Wells, the Octave boundaries).

4. **Sunny's ability is recontextualized.** She doesn't "see dead people"—she perceives the Thanatological Layer's ecosystem, including Ferrymen, Mnemonic Barnacles, and Scavenger Tides. Her power is ecological perception, not spiritual mediumship.

5. **The Five's power is a beacon and a burden.** Their amplified frequency output attracts Harmonic Predators (Convergence Hunters, Amplitude Moths), feeds Frequency Parasites faster, and broadcasts their location across Octaves. Power without control is a dinner bell.

6. **Real science underpins the mythology.** The 19 Hz frequency that causes haunting sensations, the Hessdalen Lights as atmospheric plasma phenomena, Michael Persinger's research on electromagnetic fields triggering hallucinations, and the documented connection between infrasound and perceived presences—all provide grounding for the universe's frequency physics.

7. **Entities exist on a spectrum of intelligence.** From mindless Trauma Impressions (recordings) through instinct-driven Parasites and Plasmoids, to fully sapient Octave Travelers—the universe has a complete ecology, not just monsters. This creates nuanced narrative possibilities where not every encounter is adversarial.
