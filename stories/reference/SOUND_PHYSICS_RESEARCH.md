## 1. RESONANT FREQUENCY

### The Real Science

**Core Principle:** Every physical object has a natural frequency (or set of frequencies) at which it vibrates most efficiently. When an external force matches this frequency, energy transfers with maximum efficiency, causing amplitude to build dramatically. This is **resonance**.

**Key Physics:**
- Natural frequency is determined by an object's mass, stiffness, and geometry
- Formula: f = (1/2π)√(k/m) for simple systems (k = stiffness, m = mass)
- At resonance, even small periodic inputs accumulate into large oscillations
- Damping (friction, air resistance) limits how much amplitude can build
- Without damping, amplitude theoretically grows without bound

**Proven Examples:**
- **Wine glass shattering:** A trained singer (or speaker at ~550-700 Hz, depending on glass geometry) can match a crystal glass's resonant frequency. The glass vibrates with increasing amplitude until the material's elastic limit is exceeded. Requires ~100-110 dB at the exact frequency. Demonstrated repeatedly on MythBusters and in physics labs.
- **Tacoma Narrows Bridge (1940):** Often cited as resonance but actually **aeroelastic flutter** — a self-exciting feedback loop between wind, the bridge's torsional motion, and aerodynamic forces. The wind didn't "match" the bridge's frequency; the bridge's own motion altered airflow, which amplified the motion further. Key distinction: resonance requires external periodic forcing; flutter is self-exciting.
- **Millennium Bridge, London (2000):** On opening day, pedestrians' footsteps synchronized with lateral bridge oscillation (~0.5-1 Hz). True resonance — human walking frequency matched the bridge's lateral mode.
- **Soldiers breaking step on bridges:** Military tradition since at least the 1831 Broughton Suspension Bridge collapse. Marching frequency (~2 Hz) can match bridge natural frequencies.

**Critical Detail — Q Factor:**
- The "Quality factor" (Q) measures how sharp a resonance is — how precisely you must match the frequency
- Crystal glass: Q ≈ 1000 (must be very precise, within ~0.5 Hz)
- Human body organs: Q ≈ 3-10 (broad, sloppy resonance)
- Tuning fork: Q ≈ 1000-5000
- Higher Q = more dramatic resonance, but harder to achieve

**Resonant Frequencies of the Human Body (NASA Technical Report, 1977):**
- Whole body (standing): 5-8 Hz
- Head (axial): 20-30 Hz
- Eyeball: 18-20 Hz (!)
- Chest wall: 50-100 Hz
- Abdominal mass: 4-8 Hz
- Skull: ~300 Hz (varies with thickness)

### The Fringe Claims

- "Everything vibrates at a frequency and you can heal by matching it" — oversimplification of real physics
- Royal Rife's "frequency therapy" — claimed to shatter bacteria/viruses with resonant frequencies (1930s). Never replicated under controlled conditions.
- Claims that buildings/structures can be demolished with targeted sound — theoretically possible but requires enormous sustained energy at precise frequency

## 4. CYMATICS

### The Real Science

**Core Principle:** Sound makes matter move in organized patterns. Cymatics is the study of these visible sound patterns — the geometric shapes that emerge when a vibrating surface is covered with particles or fluid.

**Ernst Chladni (1787):**
- German physicist used a violin bow on metal plates dusted with sand
- Sand migrated to **nodal lines** — areas of zero vibration
- Different frequencies produced different geometric patterns
- Called "Chladni figures" — foundational to understanding vibration modes
- Published in *Entdeckungen über die Theorie des Klanges* (Discoveries in the Theory of Sound)

**How It Works:**
1. A surface (plate, membrane) is excited at a specific frequency
2. The surface develops **standing wave patterns** — regions of maximum vibration (antinodes) and zero vibration (nodes)
3. Particles (sand, salt, lycopodium powder) are pushed away from antinodes and accumulate on nodes
4. The resulting pattern is a direct visualization of the vibration mode
5. Higher frequencies → more complex patterns (more nodes)
6. Each frequency has ONE specific pattern for a given plate geometry

**In Water:**
- Faraday waves: surface standing waves form geometric patterns on vibrating liquid surfaces
- Water responds to frequency with specific wavelength patterns
- At certain amplitudes, droplets bounce on vibrating water surfaces ("walkers")
- Water cymatics shows concentric rings, star patterns, and regular polygon geometries

**Modern Applications:**
- Violin makers use Chladni patterns to evaluate plate vibration modes
- Aerospace engineers test vibration modes of components
- Acoustic design of concert halls
- Nigel Stanford's "Cymatics" music video (2014) — fire, water, sand, ferrofluid all responding to specific frequencies in real-time

**Specific Frequency-Pattern Relationships (on standard Chladni plates):**
- ~120 Hz: Simple bisection line
- ~200 Hz: Cross pattern (4 segments)
- ~350 Hz: Star pattern (6 segments)
- ~500 Hz: Complex radial patterns (8+ segments)
- ~800 Hz: Fine geometric lattice
- ~1500 Hz: Extremely intricate tessellation

## 6. ACOUSTIC LEVITATION

### The Real Science

**Core Principle:** Sound waves exert radiation pressure. At sufficient intensity and with proper geometry, this pressure can counteract gravity, suspending objects in mid-air. This is NOT theoretical — it's laboratory fact.

**How It Works:**
1. Sound waves carry momentum (photons do too, but sound's effect is larger at achievable intensities)
2. When sound reflects off an object, momentum is transferred = **acoustic radiation pressure**
3. In a standing wave field, objects are pushed toward **pressure nodes** (points of minimum pressure oscillation)
4. If the upward radiation force exceeds gravity, the object levitates
5. Stability comes from the restoring force — if the object drifts from the node, it's pushed back

**Real Experiments:**

**NASA (1975-present):**
- Patent: "Levitation of objects using acoustic energy" — activated sound source establishes standing wave pattern; solid or liquid material moves to antinodes and remains suspended
- Used acoustic levitation for containerless materials processing on Space Shuttle (STS-61A mission, 1985)
- Heated levitated samples to 600-1500°C without any container contamination
- Frequency used: typically 20-40 kHz (ultrasonic)

**University of Bristol / University of Tsukuba (2026):**
- Developed **zero-order Bessel beam** acoustic levitation
- Levitated 1.5mm polystyrene sphere at up to **40 cm distance** from sound source
- Six times farther than any previous single-sided acoustic trap
- Can manipulate objects in 3D, levitate multiple objects, and levitate non-spherical objects
- Published in *Physical Review Letters* (August 2026)

**Living Organisms Levitated:**
- Ants, ladybugs, and young fish successfully levitated ultrasonically (2006, Xie et al.)
- Insects showed no evident harm during levitation
- Fish vitality reduced due to inadequate water supply, not sound damage

**Limitations (Real Physics):**
- Current max object size: ~2cm diameter (for single-frequency systems)
- Requires ultrasonic frequencies (>20 kHz) for small objects
- Larger objects → lower frequencies needed, but MORE acoustic power required
- Energy requirement scales dramatically with mass
- Human levitation would require: ~140-160 dB at 1-5 Hz — this is roughly the sound pressure of a rocket launch, and would be lethal long before it levitated you

**The Math:**
- Acoustic radiation pressure: P = 2E/c (where E = energy density, c = speed of sound)
- To levitate 1 gram: ~0.01 N force needed
- To levitate 70 kg human: ~700 N force needed
- Required sound intensity for human levitation: ~190 dB — would instantly destroy all biological tissue

### The Fringe Claims

- "Tibetan monks levitated stones with chanting" — no evidence
- "Ancient Egyptians used sound to lift pyramid blocks" — no evidence
- "Acoustic levitation can scale to large objects easily" — physics says no (energy requirements become extreme)
- However: **multiple-frequency arrays** could theoretically extend range, and metamaterial focusing could concentrate acoustic energy

## 10. SYMPATHETIC RESONANCE

- **Sitar sympathetic strings:** 11-13 unstruck "taraf" strings vibrate in sympathy with the 6-7 played strings, creating the sitar's characteristic shimmer.

  - Source amplitude is sufficient

## 11. HARMONIC SERIES

### The Real Science

**Core Principle:** When any object vibrates, it doesn't produce a single pure frequency. It produces a **fundamental frequency** plus a theoretically infinite series of **overtones** at integer multiples of the fundamental. These are the harmonic series.

**The Series:**
If fundamental = f:
- 1st harmonic (fundamental): f (1×f)
- 2nd harmonic (1st overtone): 2f
- 3rd harmonic (2nd overtone): 3f
- 4th harmonic: 4f
- 5th harmonic: 5f
- ...continues infinitely

**Example (A = 110 Hz):**
- 110, 220, 330, 440, 550, 660, 770, 880, 990, 1100... Hz

**Why This Matters:**
- **Timbre** (tone color) is determined by which harmonics are present and their relative amplitudes
- A trumpet and a violin playing the same note (same fundamental) sound different because their harmonic profiles differ
- The human voice's unique character comes from formants — resonances of the vocal tract that amplify certain harmonics

**Musical Relationships Within the Series:**
| Harmonic Ratio | Interval | Musical Relationship |
|----------------|----------|---------------------|
| 2:1 | Octave | Same note, higher |
| 3:2 | Perfect Fifth | Most consonant interval |
| 4:3 | Perfect Fourth | Second most consonant |
| 5:4 | Major Third | Sweet, bright |
| 6:5 | Minor Third | Warm, slightly dark |
| 7:4 | Harmonic Seventh | Slightly flat minor seventh |

**Pythagoras and the Monochord:**
- Pythagoras (6th century BCE) discovered that pleasing musical intervals correspond to simple integer ratios
- A string divided in half (2:1) gives an octave
- Divided into thirds (3:2) gives a fifth
- This was the first known connection between mathematics and aesthetics

**Inharmonicity:**
- Real vibrating objects don't produce perfectly harmonic overtones
- Piano strings, for example, have slightly sharp upper partials (inharmonicity caused by string stiffness)
- Bells produce inharmonic partials — which is why they have that distinctive "clangy" quality
- Inharmonicity is what separates "musical" sounds from "noise" — noise has random frequency content

**The Infinite Series:**
- Every note contains, in principle, ALL frequencies above it (at decreasing amplitudes)
- A single vibrating string is simultaneously vibrating at its fundamental AND all harmonics
- The string literally contains infinite information in one vibration

- "Overtone singing accesses spiritual realms" — overtone singing (Tuvan throat singing) is remarkable acoustic skill, not metaphysics

## 14. STANDING WAVES

### The Real Science

**Core Principle:** When sound waves reflect between boundaries (walls, ends of tubes/strings) and overlap with themselves, they create **standing waves** — patterns where certain points always have maximum vibration (antinodes) and certain points always have zero vibration (nodes). These patterns are fixed in space.

**How They Form:**
1. Sound wave travels forward → hits boundary → reflects
2. Reflected wave travels backward → meets incoming wave
3. At certain frequencies (resonant modes), forward and reflected waves reinforce perfectly
4. Result: apparent "standing" pattern — nodes and antinodes don't move
5. These frequencies are determined by the space's dimensions

**Room Modes:**
- Every enclosed space has resonant frequencies based on its dimensions
- Formula: f = c/(2L) × n, where c = speed of sound, L = room dimension, n = mode number
- A 5-meter room has its first axial mode at: 343/(2×5) = 34.3 Hz
- Types of room modes:
  - **Axial:** between two parallel surfaces (1D)
  - **Tangential:** between four surfaces (2D)
  - **Oblique:** between all six surfaces (3D)

**The Dead Spot / Hot Spot Problem:**
- At a **node**: sound at that frequency cancels completely — a "dead spot" where you literally can't hear certain notes
- At an **antinode**: sound is doubled in amplitude — a "hot spot" that's disproportionately loud
- This is why music studios spend thousands on acoustic treatment
- Below ~250 Hz, room modes are widely spaced and create severe unevenness
- Above ~250 Hz, modes pack together closely enough that they smooth out

**Real-World Manifestations:**
- Concert halls: careful geometry prevents standing wave buildup at seat positions
- Shower singing: your bathroom has strong resonant modes, which is why certain notes "bloom" and sustain — you've hit a room mode
- Automotive "boom" — that one frequency that makes the whole car vibrate on the highway
- Whispering galleries (St. Paul's Cathedral): curved surfaces focus sound along predictable paths

**Standing Waves in Instruments:**
- Every musical instrument works by establishing standing waves (in strings, air columns, or plates)
- Open pipe (flute): antinodes at both ends, all harmonics present
- Closed pipe (clarinet): node at closed end, only odd harmonics present — explains the distinct clarinet timbre
- String instruments: nodes at fixed ends, antinodes in between

**The Helmholtz Resonator:**
- An enclosed volume with a narrow opening (like blowing across a bottle)
- Has ONE powerful resonant frequency
- Formula: f = (c/2π) × √(A/(V×L)) where A = opening area, V = volume, L = neck length
- Applications: bass traps in studios, car exhaust tuning, the ocarina

- "Certain rooms trap negative energy at standing wave frequencies" — conflation of acoustic dead spots with spiritual concepts
- "Ancient temples were designed as standing wave generators" — some were acoustically remarkable (Maltese Hypogeum resonates at 111 Hz), but attributing intent is speculative

## 15. THE COCKTAIL PARTY EFFECT

### The Real Science

**Core Principle:** The human brain can selectively attend to one voice among many competing voices — even when all voices are at similar volume and frequency. This is called the "cocktail party effect" (Colin Cherry, 1953).

**MIT Research (2026):**
- McDermott Lab computational model of the auditory system
- Found that **multiplicative gains** in neural processing units explain selective attention
- How it works:
  1. Brain receives cue (features of target voice: pitch, timbre, location)
  2. Neurons tuned to target features get "scaled up" (multiplied by factor >1)
  3. Neurons NOT tuned to target get suppressed (multiplied by factor <1)
  4. Result: target voice emerges from noise
- Key finding: this simple multiplicative mechanism reproduces "a very wide range of human attentional behaviors for sound"
- In low-level auditory cortex: attention *modulates* — enhances target but ignored speech remains represented
- In higher-order regions: representation becomes *selective* — no detectable tracking of ignored speech

**What the Brain Uses to Separate Voices:**
| Feature | How It Helps | Brain Region |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| Pitch (F0) | Each voice has unique fundamental frequency | Primary auditory cortex |
| Timbre | Vocal tract shape creates unique spectral profile | Secondary auditory cortex |
| Spatial location | Interaural time/level differences | Superior olivary complex |
| Speech rhythm | Each speaker has unique temporal pattern | Left hemisphere language areas |
| Semantic context | Prediction of upcoming words | Prefrontal cortex |

**The Limits:**
- Works best when voices differ in pitch by >2 semitones
- Degrades significantly with more than 3-4 competing voices
- Spatial separation helps enormously — even small angular differences
- Hearing damage impairs the cocktail party effect disproportionately (one of the first things to go in age-related hearing loss)
- The "ignored" speech is still processed to some degree — your name breaks through (the "own-name effect")

**Attention as a Neural Filter:**
- The brain essentially has a tunable bandpass filter for attention
- Attended features get gain-boosted; unattended features get attenuated
- This filtering happens at multiple stages of processing
- Beta-band cortical oscillations enhance during selective attention tasks
- The brain is doing real-time spectral analysis AND selective amplification — essentially functioning as a highly sophisticated audio mixer

### RULES FOR WRITING POWERS (PHYSICS CONSISTENCY)

1. **Energy conservation:** Powers require energy. Bigger effects = more exhaustion. Breaking a glass is easy; breaking a building takes minutes of sustained effort.

2. **Inverse square law (in air):** Sound intensity drops with the square of distance. Double the distance = quarter the power. This applies to ALL characters in open air.

3. **Frequency-range tradeoff:** Low frequencies travel far but carry little information. High frequencies carry detail but die quickly. Characters must choose between REACH and PRECISION.

4. **Medium matters:** Powers work differently in every environment. Water amplifies range. Steel conducts instantly. Rubber deadens everything. Open air disperses.

5. **Standing waves shape everything:** In enclosed spaces, room geometry creates zones of amplification and cancellation. Characters MUST account for this or their powers behave unexpectedly.

6. **Harmonics create side effects:** Any frequency produced inherently generates overtones. A character targeting one effect will ALWAYS have secondary unintended effects at harmonic frequencies.

7. **Damping is the enemy:** Soft, absorbent materials (flesh, fabric, foam, earth) resist resonance. Rigid materials (glass, steel, crystal) resonate eagerly. This determines target vulnerability.

8. **Phase coherence is the superpower:** Normal sound spreads chaotically. What makes Signal Decay characters special isn't VOLUME — it's COHERENCE. They maintain perfect phase relationships over distance, preventing destructive interference. This is what separates them from a really loud speaker.

9. **Biological systems resist:** Living tissue is highly damped (Q < 10). Powers that work on rigid matter (Jude) struggle against flesh. Powers that work on minds (Kael, Oren) must use different mechanisms than powers that work on matter.

10. **The Schumann anchor:** 7.83 Hz is the world's baseline. Characters in tune with it are grounded. Characters who lose sync become unstable. This is Signal Decay's version of "the Force" — a physical, measurable field that connects everything.
