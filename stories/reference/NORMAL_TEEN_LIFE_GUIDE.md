# NORMAL TEEN LIFE GUIDE — Signal Decay High School Grounding
## Reference: What each character's NORMAL life looks like (ages 14-18)
## Created: 2026-08-26
## Purpose: The powers are background noise. THIS is the real story.

---

> **RULE: The supernatural is 20% of the high school experience. The other 80% is just BEING A TEENAGER.** Crushes. Heartbreak. Parties. First kiss. Sports. Prom. Friend drama. Figuring out who you are. The powers are weird moments WITHIN that normal life — not the center of it.

## KAEL VOSS (HS: 2016-2020) — The Silent Boy Who Everyone Feels Something About

### Sports & Activities
- **Basketball** — JV, then varsity bench. Not a star but has incredible court vision. Communicates entirely through body language on court. Coach says: "Best non-verbal player I've ever had. Reads the game like he already knows what's happening."
- No other extracurriculars. Not a joiner. But shows up to Troy's stuff (Troy's in drama club — Kael sits in the back during rehearsals, silently supportive).

### Social Life
- **Troy** is everything. Best friends since 6th grade. They have routines — wings on Thursdays, video games on weekends, silently watching movies together. Troy learned to read Kael's micro-expressions, his gestures, his one-word texts. Their friendship functions like a marriage in terms of non-verbal communication.
- **Not a loner.** People gravitate toward him. His silence isn't off-putting — it's MAGNETIC. He's invited to things. He usually goes. He just doesn't talk.
- Small friend group: Troy + 3-4 others who've accepted the silence. They protect him from people who push.

### Girls & Romance
- Girls are INTERESTED. He's lean, watchful, has those eyes. The "mysterious" thing works on teenage girls HARD.
- **Girlfriend 1 (sophomore year):** Lasted 2 months. A girl named Destiny who liked the idea of him more than the reality. She needed WORDS — "tell me you like me," "say something romantic." He couldn't. She cried. He felt like shit. They ended amicably.
- **Girlfriend 2 (junior year):** Maya. Lasted 5 months. This one was better — she communicated through touch, through proximity. They held hands constantly. Made out in her car. She learned to read his texts as love language. But eventually: "I need you to SAY it, Kael. I need to hear it." He couldn't. She left. This one hurt.
- **The party kiss (junior year):** A girl he barely knew grabbed him during a house party and kissed him. He kissed back. It was good. She wanted to talk about it Monday at school. He avoided her for two weeks. She moved on.
- **Prom:** Goes with Troy (not romantic — just as friends because neither has a date). Doesn't dance. Stands by the wall in a suit that fits perfectly (his mom picked it, she has taste). Watches the room. One moment: a slow song plays and a girl — someone he's never talked to — walks up, takes his hand, and just SWAYS with him. No words. 3 minutes. She smiles and walks away after. Best 3 minutes of his high school life. He doesn't know her name. Never finds out.

### The Vibe
- Other kids describe him as: "He's like a character in a movie who knows more than everyone else but can't tell you." "It's weird — I always want to know what he's thinking." "You feel SAFE around him. Like nothing bad can happen if he's there."
- Teachers either love him (English, art — where silence is valued) or are frustrated (participation grades kill him).
- He's not SAD about the silence most of the time. He's adapted. It's just life. The sadness hits in specific moments — when Maya asks him to say it, when his mom looks worried, when he WANTS to shout something at a game and his throat closes.

## MIRA CHEN (HS: 2018-2022) — The Art Girl Who Sees Too Much

### Social Life
- **Sage** is her person. Ride-or-die since 8th grade. Sleepovers every weekend. They finish each other's sentences. Sage doesn't know about the entities — she just thinks Mira is "artistic and weird" and loves her for it.
- Small circle: Sage + 2-3 other art kids. They eat lunch in the art room. Ms. Brennan (art teacher) lets them stay after school. The art room is SAFE SPACE.
- NOT bullied — just EXISTS on the periphery. People know who she is. Nobody targets her. Nobody really tries to get close either (except Sage).

### Art & Expression
- Paints CONSTANTLY. Her room is floor-to-ceiling canvases. Parents worry ("this isn't a career, Mira"). She ignores them. Can't stop. The painting isn't a choice — it's a NEED. When she doesn't paint for 3+ days, the entities get LOUDER.
- Gets into a local gallery show at 17 (youth exhibition). Sells one painting for $200. Her dad's attitude shifts slightly. Still worried. But shifted.

### Girls & Sexuality
- **The Sage kiss (sophomore year):** Truth or Dare at a sleepover with 4 other girls. Someone dares Mira to kiss Sage. She does. It's a REAL kiss. 5 seconds. Sage laughs it off. Mira doesn't sleep that night. Thinks about it for weeks. Doesn't pursue it — too much else going on in her head (entities, fear, confusion about WHAT she is).
- Files it under "later." Is she bi? Gay? She doesn't know. Doesn't have bandwidth to figure it out while also managing invisible beings in her peripheral vision 24/7.
- **One date with a boy (junior year):** Kevin from AP Bio. Goes to a movie. Holds hands. She likes him fine. But the whole time she's seeing his frequency aura and it's... yellow-green. Anxious. He's nervous and she can SEE it radiating off him like heat shimmer. It's distracting. She doesn't go on a second date. Tells Sage: "He was nice but I couldn't focus." Sage: "On what?" Mira: "...the movie."
- **Doesn't date seriously in HS.** The entities make intimacy too complicated. She can't relax around people enough to let them in.

### The Party (Junior Year)
- ONE big party. Sage drags her. First time drinking (vodka cranberry, then another, then another).
- Alcohol loosens her suppression of Chromatic Sight. She starts SEEING auras on everyone. And drunk-Mira has no filter:
  - To a girl: "You're lying. Your color just went orange." Girl: "...what?"
  - To a couple: "You don't love her. Your frequencies don't match." Guy: "Excuse me?"
  - To Sage: "You're pink right now. That means—" Sage: "Okay we're leaving."
- Sage drives her home. They never talk about it. People at school Monday think she was just wasted and weird. Nobody takes it seriously. But two of the things she said were RIGHT — the couple broke up a week later, the girl WAS lying about where she'd been.

### Prom
- Doesn't go. Stays home. Tells Sage: "Dances aren't my thing." (Real reason: 200+ people in one room with music + her entities + alcohol = sensory overload she can't risk in public.)
- Paints all night instead. Produces the most beautiful/terrifying piece she's ever made — five figures standing in amber light. A landscape that doesn't exist on Earth (the pocket? The Overtone plane?). She doesn't know why she painted it. Titles it "Convergence" and puts it in the closet.
- It resurfaces years later. After Episode 1. After the night they meet. Mira finds it in her old stuff and goes cold. Because she painted it at 17 — before she knew them. Before they existed as a group. And it's THEM. Five figures. Amber light. Exactly right.

## JUDE OKAFOR (HS: 2014-2018) — The Big Immigrant Kid Finding His Sound

### Family & Culture
- Nigerian household. Igbo. Church on Sundays (Redeemed Christian Church of God — the Nigerian megachurch denomination). Dad drives rideshare 12 hours a day. Mom does hospital cleaning + private home care. Sister Adanma is the GOLDEN CHILD — pre-med, straight A's, parents' dream.
- Jude is expected to follow Adanma's path. He won't. This creates TENSION from age 15 onward. Not explosive — just cold. Disappointed looks. "Your sister never gave us trouble." He can't explain that his body wants to MAKE THINGS, not study things.
- Food: Mom's jollof rice is the best thing in the world. Jude can cook it by 16 (she taught him). The kitchen is the one place the family doesn't fight.

### Sports
- **Football (freshman year only):** Defensive end. He's BIG and FAST. But his tackles are... wrong. Too much impact. A kid gets hurt in a scrimmage — not badly, but the coaches are nervous. Jude's hits CREATE secondary effects — the ground seems to shake, the bench vibrates. He quits before they cut him. Tells everyone: "Not my thing."
- After that: no sports. Puts the physical energy into manual work instead. Gets a job stocking shelves at 16. Later: construction site in summers.

### Music & The Crew
- Finds his people sophomore year. The metal kids. Darius (drummer, future band The Margin) is his first real friend in the scene. They bond over Architects, Killswitch Engage, Parkway Drive.
- Learns guitar on BORROWED instruments for 3 years. A different guitar every few months — whoever will lend one. He's GOOD immediately. Natural. Picks up chord shapes fast, develops the heavy right hand that will become Seismic Lock.
- Goes to shows. Underage, sneaks in through back doors Darius knows. First mosh pit at 15 — Jude in a pit is a WALL. People bounce off him. He doesn't move. It's the first time his body feels RIGHT in a social context.
- The grant rejection (15): Ashley Cole's First Note Foundation. Writes the essay with Adanma's help. "I want to play guitar because when I play, the ground feels SOLID." Perfect application. Rejected. Form letter. No explanation. He applies to three more — all rejected. Doesn't get his OWN guitar until 18 (Darius gives him an old Ibanez as a graduation gift).

### Girls & Romance
- **Not confident about it freshman/sophomore year.** He's big, he's Nigerian, he's still carrying an accent he's trying to lose. Girls like him by junior year once he fills out and finds his swagger.
- **Amara (senior year):** Nigerian-American. Same church. They bond over shared culture + shared frustration with parent expectations. She's studying business. He's playing guitar. They argue about "realistic careers" but it's affectionate. She comes to his garage jams. Thinks he's incredible.
- First time they hook up is after a house show where he played (borrowed guitar, borrowed amp, 4-song set). She's waiting for him after. "That was... I FELT that." They're together 8 months.
- The relationship ends when he gets arrested at 19 (just after graduation). She visits twice at the facility. Then stops. He doesn't blame her. But it breaks something in him about trust.

### Parties & Social
- DJs parties by junior/senior year. Hooks up a Bluetooth speaker and plays his curated mix — metalcore breakdowns into hip-hop beats into Nigerian afrobeats. People trust his taste. He's the vibe-setter.
- Drinks at parties but doesn't get DRUNK. He's seen what losing control does (the wall-crack incident). Keeps it to 2-3 beers.
- Prom: Goes with Amara. She wears gold. He wears all black. They look incredible together. They dance. He actually DANCES — and the floor vibrates with every step but people just think it's the bass from the speakers. It's not. It's him. Amara feels it and pulls him closer. Best night of his life (until it's not — 4 months later).

## ZARA KOVAČ (HS: 2015-2019) — The Alien Pretending To Be The Cool Smart Girl

### Family
- Croatian dad (Ante) + Black American mom (Jasmine). Working class. Dad does HVAC repair. Mom is a receptionist at a dental office. They met at a bar in 1990. Still in love — embarrassingly so. The house is WARM even when money is tight.
- Brother **Marko** (4 years younger) — hyperactive, sporty, annoying in the way little brothers are. She protects him instinctively.
- Sister **Lena** (8 years younger) — the baby. Zara feels something FROM Lena — a frequency familiarity — that she can't explain. She's drawn to her sister in a way that transcends normal sibling bond. Lena is her frequency complement. She doesn't know this consciously yet.
- Dad's cassette tapes of Croatian folk music — Zara listens to them DIFFERENTLY than anyone else. She hears the frequency architecture, not just the melody. One tape has a note at 347 Hz buried in the background of a recording from 1983. She's been replaying that section since age 11.

### Social Life
- She's POPULAR in the weird way where everyone likes her but nobody KNOWS her. She's funny. She's smart. She's good at parties. But:
  - Best friend? Doesn't have one. Has 15 people who think they're close to her.
  - They're not. She's performing "normal human friendship" at all times. It's EXHAUSTING.
- The girl she tried to tell (age 15): Sofia. Closest she got to a real friend. Spanish class together. Sofia said "you're like an old soul" and Zara almost broke. Tried: "I'm not really from here. I remember another existence." Sofia: "That's such a cool metaphor, Zara. Very poetic." Zara never tried again.

### Sexuality & Dating
- She's QUEER — attracted to FREQUENCY, not gender/appearance. A person's voice in the right key. A laugh that harmonizes with her baseline. Boy or girl, doesn't matter. It's about the SOUND of them.
- **Elena (sophomore year):** Art student. Plays viola. They date for 3 months. Zara is drawn to her because the viola's frequency is close to her own home-frequency. It ends because Zara can't be CLOSE without Elena sensing something's different. "It's like you're somewhere else even when you're here." Zara: "I know." They stay friends.
- **A boy (junior year):** Marcus. Voice like warm bass. She dates him for 5 weeks purely because his speaking voice is at 98 Hz and it makes her feel HOME. He's boring otherwise. She breaks up gently. He's confused. She can't explain: "Your voice was the only interesting thing about you" is too mean to say.
- **Prom:** Goes. Wears a SUIT — charcoal, fitted, open collar. Looks incredible. Dances with boys AND girls because she doesn't care about the social politics of it. Stays until close. One of the best nights of her performance-of-being-human. For one night, pretending is FUN instead of painful.
- Goes home. Cries in the bathroom for 40 minutes. Because she danced with 12 people tonight and NONE of them know what she is. None of them ever will. And the one moment during a slow song where she closed her eyes and FELT the bass through the floor — for 4 seconds she was just a frequency being hearing music in a room full of vibrating human bodies — and it was beautiful and she was alone in it.

### The Sound Tech Job
- Gets hired at The Frequency at 17. Keisha Park hires her because Zara's mix during a tryout session sounds IMPOSSIBLY good. Keisha can't explain it — "You just HEAR things nobody else hears." Zara: "I've always been like that." Understatement of the millennium.
- The venue becomes her HOME. The one place where frequency is SUPPOSED to exist. Where she can be close to her real self. Where she adjusts EQ and nobody thinks it's weird that she can hear a 0.5 dB difference at 4.7 kHz without looking at the meter.

## OREN MALIK (HS: 2013-2017) — The Boy Unstuck From Time

### The Before/After
- **Before Dad's death (freshman year, first 3 months):** Normal happy kid. Chubby. Funny. Good grades (not exceptional). Plays soccer (badly — no coordination, which is ironic given what he becomes). Has friends. Is liked.
- **After (age 14.5 onward):** Everything changes. The temporal awareness begins. The loneliness sets in. He's still funny — humor as armor, like Zara — but there's something BEHIND it now. A distance. He's watching the world from slightly ahead of it.

### The Aesthetic Development (Age 16-17)
- First time wearing a skirt to school: March of junior year. Heart pounding. Bracing for impact.
- Response: mixed. Some shit-talking from the expected sources. But also: girls LOVING it. "Oren you look amazing." "WHERE did you get those socks?" He finds that the positive outweighs the negative.
- By senior year it's just WHO HE IS. Platform shoes. Dresses. Thigh-high socks. Thick glasses (he needs them — not aesthetic). Fluffy dark hair. He's small and round and cute and people either get it or they don't.
- The school administration calls his mom ONCE. "Is Oren... is there something...?" Fatima (his mom, Egyptian-American, scientist): "My son wears what makes him comfortable. Is there a dress code violation?" There wasn't. Never called again.

### Friends
- **Hass** — the chaos agent. Met in 9th grade English. Hass is the only person who consistently surprises Oren. Their friendship is built on Hass being UNPREDICTABLE — doing things that Oren's 0.3-second precognition can't anticipate. This makes Hass precious. The only person who doesn't feel scripted.
- **Ravi** — the smart one. Pre-med. Indian-American. Analyzes everything. He's the one who notices Oren's "extra hands" pattern in drum critiques and files it as clinically interesting. They study together (Ravi because he needs to, Oren because he likes Ravi's company).
- Small but loyal group. They protect each other.

### Sexuality
- **Comes out as bi/fluid junior year.** It's not dramatic — he just starts being open about it. His friends already knew.
- **Naveed (junior year):** Pakistani boy. Also from a Muslim family. Deeply closeted. They have a thing for 2 months — secret. Oren refuses to be anyone's secret. "I'm not going back in any closet." They end. It's sad but Oren knows: he can't be with someone who's ashamed of what they are. (He doesn't see the irony — HE is hiding the temporal thing from everyone.)
- **Crushes on girls too.** Never dates one in HS — the timing (ha) never works out. There's a girl senior year who he watches from across the cafeteria. She plays cello. Her bow movements are in 7/8 and he NOTICES. He never talks to her.

### The Temporal Isolation
- By junior year he's pulling away from most people. Not from sadness — from BOREDOM. Everyone is predictable when you live 0.3 seconds ahead.
- He knows what teachers will say before they say it. He knows the punchline before the joke lands. He knows who's about to walk through a door 2 seconds before they appear.
- He can never be SURPRISED. Birthday parties are fake — he knew about every one. Christmas presents are anticlimactic. Even arguments feel scripted because he's already heard the other person's next point.
- The only antidotes: Hass (genuinely unpredictable), live music (the beat gives him a NOW to lock onto), and the ER (which he discovers at 18 when he starts volunteering — chaos and urgency override the precognition because too many variables happen at once).

### Prom
- Goes in a DRESS. Purple satin. Tea-length. Thigh-high Chinese socks (embroidered — his favorite pair). Platform shoes add 4 inches. He looks like a doll. A beautiful round-faced doll with thick glasses and fluffy hair.
- Actually has FUN. Dances all night. The DJ is playing at 128 BPM and for the first time in years, Oren's temporal drift doesn't matter because the music has a BEAT and he can lock onto it and be IN THE NOW. The beat is a metronome pulling him back to the present. He's actually HERE. Not 0.3 seconds ahead. HERE.
- It's the closest he's been to happy since his dad died.
- Last song ends. The crowd cheers. The future rushes back in. He sees everyone's next move before they make it. He's ahead again. The brief window of NOW closes.
- Goes home. Sits on his bed in the purple dress. Stares at the ceiling. Thinks: *I need to find something that makes me feel like that all the time. Something with a beat. Something that keeps me HERE.*
- He doesn't realize yet that the answer is drums.

## RULES FOR WRITING HIGH SCHOOL SCENES:

1. **80/20 rule: 80% normal teen stuff, 20% powers.** The powers are interruptions in a normal life — not the center of it.
2. **Each character has a SPECIFIC social position** — not generic "outcast." Kael is magnetic-silent. Mira is art-peripheral. Jude is immigrant-finding-his-crew. Zara is popular-but-unknown. Oren is visible-and-different.
3. **Romance is REAL and MESSY** — not love-at-first-sight. Awkward. Confusing. Short-lived. Full of miscommunication.
4. **Parties happen.** Drinking happens. Bad decisions happen. Sneaking out happens. Getting caught happens.
5. **PROM is a touchstone for every character** — it means something different to each one.
6. **Friend dynamics > romance** — the most important relationships are FRIENDSHIPS (Troy, Sage, Hass, Darius). Romance comes and goes. Best friends stay.
7. **The Legacy is ALWAYS in the background** — a van parked near the school, a "new transfer student" who's actually a field observer, a teacher who files unusual incident reports to an unknown email address.
8. **NEAR-MISSES between the five** — they almost meet multiple times. The universe is trying to bring them together. But it's not time yet.
