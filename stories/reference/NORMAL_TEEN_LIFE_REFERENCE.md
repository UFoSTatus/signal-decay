# NORMAL TEEN LIFE — Reference for High School Years Writing
## Created: 2026-08-26
## Purpose: Grounding document — powers are BACKGROUND, normal teen life is FOREGROUND

---

## RULE: The abilities are disruptions in an otherwise NORMAL teen life. Not the center of it.

These kids have to feel REAL first. The supernatural is the spice — the meal is: crushes, heartbreak, awkward dances, trying weed for the first time, first kiss, getting drunk, sneaking out, sports, friend drama, sexuality discovery, body image, family pressure, finding your crew, figuring out who the fuck you are.

---

## KAEL (2016-2020) — Normal Life

- **Sports:** Plays JV basketball. Not a star — but good court vision. Coach loves him. "Best silent player I've ever had." Communication through body language on court.
- **Friends:** Troy is his lifeline since middle school. Wings every Thursday. Video games. Normal best-friend shit.
- **Girls:** They're INTERESTED (lean, mysterious, those eyes). Had 2 girlfriends — both ended because they needed WORDS. A girl kissed him at a party junior year. He kissed back. Good. Then she wanted to talk and he couldn't. She cried. He felt like shit.
- **Prom:** Goes. Doesn't dance (can't ask anyone). Stands by the wall with Troy. Watches. Leaves early. But — one moment: a slow song, a girl grabs his hand and just SWAYS with him. No words needed. 3 minutes. She smiles and walks away. Best 3 minutes of high school.
- **Parties:** Goes occasionally (Troy drags him). Stands near the wall. Drinks one beer slowly. Leaves by 11. People know him as the quiet one. They respect his space.
- **What he does alone:** Watches YouTube (music, not tutorials — full concerts, live performances). Takes long walks at night. Runs. He runs A LOT — the physical exertion is the only thing that quiets the vibration in his chest.

## MIRA (2018-2022) — Normal Life

- **Art:** Paints constantly. Room covered in canvases. Parents worry (Chinese parents — "art isn't a career, Mira"). She applies to art school secretly. Gets in. Doesn't tell her mom until May of senior year.
- **Friends:** Sage is her PERSON. Inseparable. Sleepovers, horror movies, sharing headphones on the bus. Small tight circle (3-4 girls). They're the "weird artsy table" at lunch.
- **Sexuality:** Questioning. Kissed Sage once sophomore year during truth or dare. Felt something. Didn't pursue it. Files it away. By senior year she identifies as bi but only tells Sage.
- **Parties:** Goes to ONE party junior year. Gets drunk for the first time. Says things she shouldn't (frequency-vision, not filter). "You're lying." "You don't love her." People think she's wasted. Sage drives her home. They never speak of it.
- **Prom:** Doesn't go. Stays home. Paints all night. Produces the most beautiful/terrifying piece — five figures in amber light. Doesn't know why.
- **Music:** Gets the guitar at 16 (thrift store). Plays alone in her room. Learns from YouTube. Doesn't join school band. Doesn't perform. The guitar is PRIVATE — her and the entities.
- **What she does alone:** Paints. Plays guitar. Walks the neighborhood sketching things she sees in her peripheral vision. Fills notebooks.

## JUDE (2014-2018) — Normal Life

- **Sports:** Football freshman year (too aggressive — coaches nervous). Quits. Tries track (shot put — naturally STRONG). Does okay. Doesn't love it.
- **Friends:** Finds his crew sophomore year — the metalheads + Black kids who play instruments. Darius (drummer) is his closest. They build a friend-group around music. Go to shows (sneak in underage). Mosh pits.
- **Girls:** Popular by junior year (big, gentle, protective). Serious girlfriend senior year — AMARA (Nigerian-American). They bond over culture, music, food. She's the first person he plays guitar for. She thinks he's amazing. Relationship ends when he's arrested at 19. She visits twice in prison. Then stops.
- **Parties:** DJs them. Hooks up Bluetooth speaker. People trust his taste (metalcore/hip-hop blend). He's the vibe-setter.
- **Family pressure:** Expected to follow sister Adanma (pre-med). Parents want stability. "Music isn't a job." He sneaks playing time. Lies about where he is ("at Darius's studying").
- **The grant rejection:** Age 15. Writes the essay himself. "I want to play guitar because when I play, the ground feels SOLID." Rejected. Form letter. Doesn't get his OWN guitar until 18 (Darius gives him an old Ibanez). That chip drives everything.
- **Prom:** Goes with Amara. Actually DANCES. Has a great time. Normal. Beautiful. They take photos. She wears gold. He wears black. They look incredible.

## ZARA (2015-2019) — Normal Life

- **Academics:** Straight A's without trying. "Zara should apply to MIT." She won't — can't be that visible. Takes AP everything. Bored.
- **Friends:** Wide circle (everyone likes her humor) but NO deep connections. She's friends with everyone and truly close to no one. The loneliest popular person.
- **Sexuality:** Queer — attracted to people based on frequency, not gender. Dates Elena (art student, viola player) briefly sophomore year. Ends because Zara can't be CLOSE without them sensing something's off. Elena: "It's like you're somewhere else even when you're here." They stay friends.
- **Family:** Protects Marko (brother, 4 years younger). Watches Lena (sister, 8 years younger) for glimmers of frequency sensitivity. Helps parents (Croatian dad, Black American mom) with translating/navigating systems. The responsible one.
- **Parties:** Goes. She's FUNNY — dry roasts that make people scream. Everyone wants her at the table. But leaves early EVERY time. "I've got work." (She doesn't. She's exhausted from performing "normal.")
- **Prom:** Goes in a SUIT (not a dress). Looks incredible. Dances with multiple people — boys and girls. Stays until close. For one night, pretending is fun. Goes home and cries for 40 minutes because she can't tell anyone why she's different.
- **Music:** Bass at 16 (chosen deliberately — felt the residue). Gets the venue job at 17. The Frequency becomes her HOME. First place she feels like herself.
- **The attempt to tell someone:** Age 15. Her close friend Yara. "I'm not really from here." Yara: "That's such a cool metaphor, Zara." Subject changed. Never tried again.

## OREN (2013-2017) — Normal Life

- **Dad's death:** Start of freshman year. EVERYTHING changes. Before: normal happy kid. After: the temporal awareness, the isolation, the "always ahead" feeling.
- **Style evolution:** 16-17 — first skirt to school. First dress. First platform shoes. TERRIFYING first day. Some shit-talking. Some support (Hass threatens anyone who says anything). By senior year it's just him. "That's Oren."
- **Sexuality:** Openly bi/fluid by junior year. Brief boyfriend — Naveed (Pakistani, closeted, scared). Ends when Naveed can't be public. Oren: "I'm not going back in any closet." Cordial after. Crush on a girl senior year.
- **Friends:** Hass is his chaos agent. They do EVERYTHING together. Concerts. Late nights. Stupid adventures. Hass is the only person who can still SURPRISE him (which is why they're friends — Oren can predict everyone else).
- **Academics:** Perfect grades without studying (precognition = knows what's on the test before he sees it). Teachers think he's gifted. He thinks he's cheating.
- **Parties:** Goes if Hass drags him. Stays by the wall. Critiques the music. "That DJ is trainwrecking." People laugh. He's the funny music-snob friend. Drinks water (doesn't like how alcohol messes with his time-sense — feels like lag).
- **Sports:** Doesn't play. WATCHES. Calls every play before it happens. Friends stop inviting him to watch games. "He ruins it."
- **Prom:** Goes in a PURPLE DRESS. Thigh-highs. Platforms. Looks incredible. Dances ALL NIGHT. Actually HAS FUN. The music has a beat and he's IN it and for once the beat is NOW and he's PRESENT. Closest to happy since his dad died. Last song ends. Future rushes back. He's 0.3 seconds ahead again.
- **The drums:** Obsession develops post-dad's-death. YouTube, concerts, critiquing. The "extra hands" thing is fully established by 16. He's the school's drum expert who's never touched a kit. People find it hilarious. He finds it agonizing.

## UNIVERSAL TEEN EXPERIENCES (All Five):

- First time getting drunk (different reactions: Kael gets quieter, Mira says too much, Jude gets louder, Zara maintains perfect composure, Oren loses his time-sense and it scares him)
- First real heartbreak (not just "we broke up" but the gut-punch kind)
- A teacher who SAW them (one adult who noticed something special but couldn't name it)
- The group chat (all have one — different friend groups, different vibes)
- A song that changed them (one specific song that hit them at the right time and made them feel less alone)
- Summer jobs (Kael: grocery store. Mira: camp counselor. Jude: his dad's friend's moving company. Zara: the venue. Oren: hospital volunteer — starting the path)
- Sneaking out at night (Kael runs. Mira paints in the park. Jude jams in Darius's garage. Zara walks the city feeling frequencies. Oren walks and exists in the future for hours — peaceful in the dark.)
- The college question (Kael: doesn't go. Mira: art school. Jude: was going to community college, then prison. Zara: could go anywhere, chooses to stay local. Oren: nursing school.)

---

## THE KEY: Powers Are Parenthetical

A reader should be able to read the high school years and think "these are just KIDS" with occasional weird moments. The powers aren't the story at this age. The story is: five teenagers becoming who they'll be. The powers are just static in the background — occasional disruptions that nobody (including them) fully understands yet.

The HUMAN stuff is what makes the reader CARE. Then when the powers escalate later — we're invested in these PEOPLE, not just these ABILITIES.
