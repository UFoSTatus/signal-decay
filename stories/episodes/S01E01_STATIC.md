*[SOUND: The sub-harmonic pulse again. Slightly louder. Directional — coming from somewhere OUTSIDE the apartment. Northeast.]*

*The microwave clock advances: 4:13.*

*He picks up the glass. Drinks. Sets it down. His hand is perfectly steady.*

*But his eyes go to the window. Northeast. Into the dark city. Toward something he can't name.*

*[SOUND: The pulse FADES. Replaced by the ordinary hum of a refrigerator. An apartment returning to normal.]*

*He won't need the alarm.*
