## 1. EPISODE OVERVIEW

**Logline:** On Friday night at The Frequency, the five Harmonics — strangers drawn to the same venue all week — experience a catastrophic 3.2-second synchronization event at 10:02 PM that shatters glass, cracks concrete, bends time, and announces their existence to the cosmos. In the aftermath, they find each other, share their impossible truths, and are told by Zara — the only one who already understands — to come back tomorrow for answers. Meanwhile, Legacy field agent Corinne Zhao witnesses the event, chooses to file a false report protecting them, and the organization's unnamed leadership activates Dampener prototypes in response.

## 2. ACT STRUCTURE

### COLD OPEN (9:57 PM)
**The Watcher.** We open OUTSIDE The Frequency — in a white surveillance van across the street. Agent Corinne Zhao (34, Chinese-American) monitors five frequency signatures on classified equipment. For three weeks, nothing has happened. Tonight, one by one, the LEDs begin synchronizing. They accelerate. Go dark for 1.7 seconds. Then BLAZE white — cracking the equipment's housing. Smash cut to title card, then "FOUR MINUTES EARLIER."

> **Structural Note:** The cold open is the episode's thesis statement — we see the event's *measurement* before we see its *experience*. The audience knows something extraordinary is about to happen; the question becomes *what it feels like from inside.*

### ACT ONE (9:58–10:02 PM) — "The Build"
Four parallel threads converge toward the spike:

- **Kael** at the bar, hearing the room deconstruct into individual frequency streams. His body acting as a mixing board. He senses four "pulls" — compass points.
- **Zara** at the mixing board, seeing sub-20Hz activity on her analyzer (7.83Hz spikes, 0.77Hz pulse). She closes the screen and keeps working. Twenty-five years of practice.
- **Mira** against the far wall, SEEING the room packed twice — once with the living, once with hundreds of entities. Keys plays scales. The Roadie counts down. She can't leave.
- **Jude** backstage, hands visibly tremoring, dead bulbs flickering. He crosses the frequency threshold into the main room and is hit by the full field.
- **Oren** at the back wall, air-drumming a 0.77Hz rhythm identical to Zara's finger-tapping — synchronized without either knowing.

The act builds through a second-by-second countdown from 10:01:47 to 10:02:00, each Harmonic reaching their threshold. Zara grips the fader to BRACE. She's felt this before.

### ACT TWO (10:02 PM) — "The Spike"
**3.2 seconds. Stretched to 30 seconds of screen time.**

The convergence event depicted in exquisite slow motion for each of The Five:

| Character | Manifestation | Physical Fallout |
|---|---|---|

**The Chord:** 77.81Hz fundamental + harmonics at 116.72, 155.62, 194.53, 233.43Hz. A complete signal, whole for the first time.

**Aftermath:** The 140-person crowd experiences a collective "skip" — already rationalizing it as "the power flickered." Night Courier keeps playing. The room is POST-something.

**Zhao's Van:** Equipment destroyed. Right ear bleeding. Pentagon device cracked. The tablet displays "ACTIVATE PROTOCOL NINE." She files a false report: *"Equipment malfunction. No anomalous readings. No escalation required."* Takes a photo of the cracked device on her personal phone. The betrayal begins.

### ACT THREE (10:04–10:07 PM) — "First Contact"
The Five begin finding each other:

- **Jude → Kael:** Jude sits beside Kael at the bar. Neither speaks for ten seconds. Then: *"You felt that. Right?"* They trade confessions — glass was Kael, floor was Jude. A handshake produces an audible harmonic drone. First link established.
- **Mira** observes from across the room. Watches Zara dismiss Oren ("After the show. Find me."). Decides to approach the men first.
- **Oren → Zara:** Oren approaches the mixing position. Zara speaks without turning: *"You were tapping the same rhythm as me."* She reveals she can hear his frequency generation. Tells him to find her after the show — to "explain what you are."
- **Mira → Kael & Jude:** Mira joins the pair at the bar. *"You're one of us,"* Jude says. She confirms — she SAW the pillars. Three notes of five, humming together.

### ACT FOUR (10:12–10:42 PM) — "The Wait"
The longest act in feeling — the night winding down while five people wait for it to end:

- **Kael + Jude + Mira** sit together at the bar, sharing impossible truths in the middle of a rock show. Mira identifies Zara as *different*: "She's not waking up like we are. She's been awake."
- **Troy, Sage, Hass, Darius** each check in on their respective person, are reassured, and leave. The satellites detach one by one.
- **Oren** counts down Night Courier's remaining songs like a drummer counting in a band.
- **Zhao** in the van with backup equipment, ice on her bleeding ear, writing encrypted personal notes: *"The combined output was not additive — it was MULTIPLICATIVE. The Legacy's models are wrong."*

### ACT FIVE (10:42–11:35 PM) — "The First Chord"
Night Courier finishes. The crowd thins. Zara tells Keisha the five are "a potential booking" and gets the venue to herself.

The room empties. Five remain. Zara steps out from behind the board for the first time — out from her hiding place. Walks to the center of the room. Says: *"Come here."*

**The Reveal (abbreviated):**
- *"This building resonates at 77.81 hertz... It's YOURS. All of you. Your combined fundamental."*
- *"You're not broken. You're not sick. You're not crazy."*
- *"You're a band. You just don't know the songs yet."*

The Five unconsciously begin to harmonize — Oren taps a count-in, Kael hums the fundamental, Jude's boot vibrates the floor in rhythm, Mira air-frets to Keys' ghost piano. Zara feels it and says: *"There it is."*

She hands each a business card with her number. *"Tomorrow at noon. Here."*

They leave through the back. Stand in the parking lot under blue-shifted streetlight. Oren breaks the silence: *"So that just happened."* Mira says: *"I've never felt less alone in my life."*

They scatter into the Chicago night. Connected. Changed.

### TAG (11:58 PM) — "The Response"
**Legacy Operations Center, Sublevel 2.** An unnamed Man (silhouette only, voice of authority) reviews the data. He knows Zhao is lying — the 3.2-second EM anomaly matches their first-synchronization models exactly.

Orders:
1. Pull Zhao's biometric data from her field jacket to determine when she made her "choice."
2. Retrieve all 7 Dampener prototypes from storage — operational by Monday.
3. Wake Director Callahan.
4. Get "the Famous One's handler" — he needs to make a call.

His assessment: *"Everything we've spent thirty-five years preventing is now three weeks ahead of schedule."*

Final shot: five dots on a map, labeled H-1 through H-5, spreading back across Chicago.

*"The signal is no longer decaying. It's GROWING."*
