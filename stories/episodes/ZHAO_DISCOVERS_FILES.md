# SIGNAL DECAY — EPISODE FRAGMENT

## "The Files" — Agent Zhao's Discovery

### Placement: Season 1, Episode 4 or 5 (post-convergence, pre-confrontation)

### Created: 2026-08-25

---

*This scene occurs after Agent Corinne Zhao has been assigned to surveil Signal Decay. She's been watching them for weeks. She knows their names, their faces, their schedules. She LIKES them — more than she should. She's started listening to their music. Then she pulls their files from the Archive. And discovers that The Legacy has been watching them since before they were born.*

---

## THE SCENE

---

**INT. LEGACY ARCHIVE — SUBLEVEL 3 — NIGHT**

*Fluorescent lights hum at exactly 60 Hz — Zhao has never noticed this before, but tonight it bothers her. The sound is wrong. Too clean. Like the building is trying to sing but can only manage one note.*

*She stands at a reinforced cabinet. ARCHIVE VAULT 7. Her keycard plus a six-digit code plus a biometric scan (left thumb, left iris). Three locks for a filing cabinet. She's never questioned this before.*

*Click. Click. Click.*

*The drawer opens. Inside: five folders. Manila. Thick. Tabs labeled in typewriter font — old. Original. Not re-filed. These are the ORIGINAL folders from 1990.*

*Tab labels:*

*HARMONIC-1. HARMONIC-2. HARMONIC-3. HARMONIC-4. HARMONIC-5.*

*She pulls HARMONIC-1 first. Opens it.*

*The first page is a frequency graph — oscilloscope output, dated December 3, 1989. A single tone isolated from a chord. 147.3 Hz. Below it, handwritten in blue ink (faded — thirty-five years old):*

> *"First tone. D3 range. Strong fundamental with unusual overtone structure — harmonic intervals suggest non-terrestrial origin encoding. Predicted incarnation: 1998-2004 CE."*

*She turns the page.*

*A hospital record. Northwestern Memorial, Chicago. January 22, 2002. Baby boy. 7 lbs, 4 oz. Name: Kael Marcus Voss.*

*She turns the page.*

*A photograph. An infant in a hospital bassinet. A normal baby. Sleeping. Tiny fists clenched. Below the photo, stamped in red:*

> *FREQUENCY MATCH CONFIRMED — 96.8% — HARMONIC-1 IDENTIFIED*

*Zhao stares at the photo. This baby is Kael. The man she's been watching for three weeks. The man who barely speaks. The man whose voice makes people MOVE without knowing why. She's been watching a 24-year-old man and she's holding a photograph of him at two days old, taken by people who already knew what he would become.*

*She turns more pages. Fast now. Years of surveillance reports. Quarterly updates. School photos — Kael at 7. At 12. At 15. His tenth birthday. His first day of high school. His mother picking him up from school. None of them know they're being photographed.*

---

*She opens HARMONIC-3. Jude.*

*The frequency graph. 98.0 Hz. The hospital record — Lagos. A photograph of a newborn who will grow up to crack foundations with a guitar. Pages and pages of observation.*

*And then — a flag. A red-tabbed insert. She opens it:*

> **DIRECTED ACTION — YEAR 2015** **SUBJECT: HARMONIC-3 (OKAFOR, JUDE)** **ACTION: Deny First Note Foundation grant application #2015-4471** **AUTHORIZATION: The Famous One (external asset, per Protocol Sigma)** **RATIONALE: Subject's stated purpose (music equipment for family ensemble) would accelerate frequency development. Recommend redirect toward non-musical employment.** **OUTCOME: Application denied. Subject's family unable to afford equipment. Subject's musical development paused for approximately 18 months.** **Note: External asset expressed discomfort with this action. Reminded of Protocol Sigma terms and consequences of non-compliance. No further objection recorded.**

*Zhao reads it twice. Three times.*

*Ashley Cole. The FAMOUS ONE. The Legacy's "external asset." She knew about the deal in abstract. Protocol Sigma — the arrangement with a Resonant who provides intelligence and cooperation in exchange for protection. She knew.*

*But she didn't know he was ACTIVELY SABOTAGING CHILDREN.*

*A fifteen-year-old boy applied for a music grant. And a man with seven Grammys — a man whose foundation EXISTS to help kids like Jude — personally ensured the application was rejected. Because a kid with a guitar is a kid whose frequency develops. And a kid whose frequency develops is a kid who gets closer to activation.*

*They stopped a child from playing music.*

---

*She opens HARMONIC-4. Zara.*

*The frequency graph. 61.7 Hz. But — wait. She flips back to the first page. Looks at the graph again. Then looks at the others — Kael's, Jude's. Something is different.*

*The others show a clean fundamental with gradually emerging overtones — a soul signature developing as the infant's body grows, like a flower opening.*

*Zara's is FULLY FORMED. At birth. Every overtone present. Every harmonic locked in. The full signature blazing at maximum from day one. No development. No emergence. Just: HERE. Complete. Conscious. Aware.*

*The analyst's note below is circled three times in red pen:*

> *"91% signature strength at birth. Dormant Harmonics register 15-30%. This subject arrived CONSCIOUS. She has known what she is since her first breath."*

*Zhao sits down. In the middle of the archive floor. Legs folded. Folders spread around her.*

*She thinks about Zara behind the sound board at The Frequency. The dry humor. The way she watches the room like she's waiting for something that's already late. The way she adjusts frequencies on her board with a precision that shouldn't be possible by ear alone. The way she looks at people — not through them, not past them — INTO them. Like she's reading something written in a language only she knows.*

*Zara has been awake her entire life. Twenty-five years. Alone with the knowledge of what she is. No one to tell. No one who'd believe her. And The Legacy — who KNEW — who had the EVIDENCE — who could have reached out at ANY point and said "you're not crazy, you're not alone, we know what you are"—*

*They watched. For twenty-five years. They watched her suffer alone.*

---

*She opens HARMONIC-5. Oren.*

*And finds the temporal anomaly report:*

> *"Subject's frequency signature arrives at the sensor before the subject physically arrives at the sensor's detection range. Subject is broadcasting FORWARD IN TIME."*

*She closes the file. Slowly.*

---

*She sits with the five folders in her lap. In the 60 Hz hum of the archive lights. In the silence of Sublevel 3 at 2 AM.*

*And she reaches into her jacket pocket. Pulls out her personal phone — not the Legacy-issued one. Her personal phone that isn't monitored. That she's been told never to bring into the building.*

*She photographs every page of HARMONIC-1.*

*Then HARMONIC-2.*

*Then HARMONIC-3.*

*Then HARMONIC-4.*

*Then HARMONIC-5.*

*Seventy-three photographs. Her hands are shaking by the last one. She puts the folders back. Closes the drawer. Three locks. Click. Click. Click.*

*She walks to the elevator. Presses UP. Stares at her reflection in the brushed steel doors.*

---

**ZHAO** *(to her reflection, barely audible)*

You were babies.

---

*Elevator arrives. Doors open. She steps in. Presses GROUND.*

*As the doors close, we hold on the ARCHIVE. The drawer. The cabinet. VAULT 7.*

*And we HEAR — barely, at the edge of perception — a chord. Five notes. A recording that's been sleeping in that drawer for thirty-five years, humming to itself in the dark. Humming the names of five people who don't know they were named before they were born.*

> **CUT TO BLACK.**

## LORE NOTES:

- This scene establishes the S-4 files' existence for the audience
- It reframes The Legacy from "scary surveillance org" to "organization that made a terrible choice about children and can't undo it"
- Zhao's phone photos become the mechanism by which she later warns Signal Decay — she has PROOF
- The directed action against Jude's grant application connects Ashley's betrayal to specific, documentable harm
- Zara's file being visibly different from the others is a visual clue for the audience (even before the reveal of her alien consciousness)
- The 60 Hz hum and Tape #14's chord at the end are sound design bookends — the scene begins and ends with frequency

## PLACEMENT OPTIONS:

**Option A: S01E04 "SURVEILLANCE"** — Midseason episode focused on The Legacy. Cold open is this scene. Rest of episode shows Zhao's assignment, her growing attachment to the band, intercutting with flashbacks to the 1989 discovery.

**Option B: S01E05 "NAMES"** — The episode where Ashley's connection to The Legacy is first hinted. This scene occurs mid-episode as Zhao investigates WHY The Legacy cares so much about a band. She pulls the files expecting surveillance data from the past few weeks. She finds DECADES.

**Option C: Season 1 Finale stinger** — Brief version of this scene (just Zhao opening the drawer, seeing the birth dates, photographing one page) as a post-credits reveal.

## SCENE STATISTICS:

- Runtime estimate: 6-8 minutes (no dialogue until final line)
- Characters present: Zhao only (isolation = tension)
- Sound design: 60 Hz hum (constant), page-turning (ASMR close), Zhao's breathing (accelerating), final chord (barely perceptible — most viewers won't consciously hear it, but will FEEL it)
- Camera: Start wide (establishing the archive). Move to CLOSE as she reads. End EXTREME CLOSE on her eyes reflected in elevator doors.
- Color: Archive is cold blue/white fluorescent. The folders are warm manila — the only warm color in the frame. The photographs inside are even warmer. The humanity stands out against the institutional coldness.
